Training Sahil: trainer’s cheat sheet

Friday 9 October · about 45 minutes · on Sahil’s own phone, app in English

For Tushar
Goal for today

Sahil places the day’s order alone — count, fill, explain, submit — and knows how to confirm a delivery and do a stock count.

Repeat these all session
  1. Count first, type second.
  2. Submit before the deadline in the grey strip.
  3. Red card: check the number, then give the honest reason.
  4. Blank = skip. 0 = deliberately none.
  5. Submit goes to the Manager, not to the supplier.
  6. Every delivery: Confirm receipt + WZ photo.
Before you start
  • Sahil has read the 2-page pre-work.
  • Sahil’s own phone, charged and online.
  • Wola code ready — dictate it, don’t write it down.
  • A real order to do together: Bukat or Intermlecz before 17:00 → delivered Saturday.
  • Today’s Bukat / Intermlecz not submitted yet — no duplicates.
  • This sheet and a pen for the sign-off on page 2.

2

Read the order screen 5 min

Say
  • Top row = suppliers; swipe sideways for more.
  • The grey strip is your deadline: delivery day + Order by … 17:00.
  • Who orders = your full name. Delivery date comes from the calendar — leave it as it is.
  • Thursday, Bukat and Intermlecz: pick for 3 days and add the extra days yourself — the suggestion doesn’t.
Check Sahil reads out the delivery day and deadline for Bukat and Intermlecz.
Top of the order screen
3

Count, then fill the card 10 min

Say
  • Count first, type second. ① Current stock = on the shelf now; pack products have two boxes (packs + loose).
  • ② Suggestion = what’s missing to reach the target. Tap it to copy it into ③ Ordering (supplier’s unit: crates, cartons…).
  • Not ordering it? Leave Ordering blank. 0 means “deliberately none”.
Do Count 4–5 real products together; Sahil types them in. Check Sahil explains one card: target − stock = need → rounded to the supplier’s unit. ④ green = matches.

4

Deviations and reasons 5 min

Say
  • More than 25% from the suggestion → card turns red. Check the number (typo? unit?); if it’s right, pick the honest reason → orange.
  • CRITICAL items: below the suggestion always needs a reason. Other reason needs a comment.
  • Bulk reason puts one reason on every red card — only when it fits all of them.
Do Sahil types a big number on purpose, picks a reason, then puts the right number back.

5

Submit and follow up 5 min

Say
  • The bottom bar says what’s missing. When it says Ready to submit, tap Submit.
  • Read the Send this order? box — especially “Critical items with nothing ordered”.
  • It goes to the Manager, who checks it and sends it to the supplier.
  • History: With manager editable · Ordered sent · Completed delivered. “changed by manager (was 5)” = the Manager corrected a line.
Do Submit the real Bukat or Intermlecz order before 17:00. Already sent today? Stop at the dialog and tap Go back and fix. Check Sahil finds the order in History as With manager.

6

Confirm a delivery 5 min

Say
  • Delivery arrived → History → the Ordered order → Confirm delivery.
  • Delivered is pre-filled with what was ordered — change only what’s different. Received by = your name.
  • Photo of the WZ. Black camera screen? Take it with the phone camera, then From gallery.
  • Confirm receipt is final — no edits afterwards.
Do Open an Ordered order and walk the screen. Tap Confirm receipt only if that delivery is really here.
Confirm delivery
7

Stock count 7 min

Say
  • ☰ → Inventory count. Count date + Counted by (your name).
  • Blank = not counted · 0 = nothing on hand.
  • Interrupted? Save draft. Finished? Approve.
  • On the next order: Fill stock from a count → Fill empty copies the counted numbers into Current stock. Check them — nothing is filled automatically.
Do Count one real category (e.g. Refrigerated) and approve it, then show Fill empty on the order screen.

8 · Quick quiz 5 min, no app
  1. Where is the deadline? Grey strip under the suppliers: “Order by … 17:00”.
  2. A card is red. What first? Check the number. If it’s right, pick the reason.
  3. Blank or 0 in Ordering? Blank = not ordering. 0 = deliberately none (may ask for a reason).
  4. Wrong quantity after Submit? Edit while With manager. Locked or Ordered → message Marek.
  5. The delivery is here. Steps? Check it, Received by, WZ photo, Confirm receipt.
  6. Thursday Bukat order? Order for 3 days — add the extra days yourself.
