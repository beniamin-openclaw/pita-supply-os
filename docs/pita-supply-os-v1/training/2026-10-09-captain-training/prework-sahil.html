Ordering stock with Supply OS

Pre-work for Friday’s training with Tushar · a 10-minute read

For Sahil

Supply OS is how we order from our suppliers. You count what’s on the shelf, the app suggests how much to order, you check it and submit. The Manager then sends it to the supplier. It runs in your phone’s browser — nothing to install.

Rounded to the supplier’s unit: 1 skrzynka (crate) = 6 kg, so 27 kg ≈ 5 crates. The tile shows it as 30 (kg) — every card shows its maths.
  1. 1Current stock — what you counted. Pack products have two boxes: whole packs + loose units.
  2. 2Suggestion — what’s missing to reach the target. Tap it to copy it into Ordering.
  3. 3Ordering — what you order, in the supplier’s unit (crates, cartons…). Not ordering it? Leave it blank.
  4. 4Status — green means your order matches the suggestion.

The app only suggests. You know the restaurant — you decide, and you explain big differences.

3 · When the app asks you why

Example, normal product: suggestion 8 → ordering 7 or 9 is fine; 5 or 12 asks for a reason.

The card turns red when
  • you counted stock and order more than 25% above or below the suggestion;
  • a CRITICAL product is ordered below the suggestion;
  • stock is left blank and the order is above the MAX.
RedReason needed
OrangeReason given
GreenMatches suggestion

Red? First check the number — a typo or the wrong unit is the usual cause. If it’s right, pick the honest reason:

Event / High traffic Weekend / High traffic Stock until next delivery Not enough storage space Packaging limitation Supplier short-delivers System suggestion is wrong Other reason + comment

Golden rules
  1. Count first, then type.
  2. Blank = not ordering. 0 means “deliberately none” and may ask for a reason.
  3. Red card? Check the number, then give the honest reason.
  4. Submit before the deadline in the grey strip.
  5. Every delivery: Confirm receipt + a photo of the WZ.
