## Findings

No substantive gap requiring revision was found. The plan resolves the difficult interactions rather than deferring them to implementation:

- Attribution is a batch snapshot, mandatory before a genuinely new insert, nullable for historical rows and exact legacy replay. It does not claim authenticated employee identity.
- Compatibility hashing omits only newly introduced null fields, retaining original nullable fields and established quantity normalization. Existing batch resolution precedes live master validation; the insert-conflict fence still handles concurrent winners.
- Curated, master and manual identities remain separate. Master snapshots resolve inside the write transaction; forged identities/units reject atomically with a shared 422 outcome. Aggregate grouping includes source identity and exact unit, avoiding accidental name-based merging.
- Arbitrary master inventory units are retained. In particular, master `g` is a base unit with decimal arithmetic, distinct from curated display grams converted to canonical kg. Existing curated/Other restrictions remain scoped to their original modes.
- Ordinary old drafts can gain an empty reporter without losing rows; uncertain frozen bodies and UUIDs remain unchanged. A 404 or rejection after a previously uncertain write cannot silently unlock a duplicate submission.
- The existing authenticated master-products endpoint and name/datalist pattern fit the request. A waste-local chooser avoids manufacturing purchasing identities or expanding the ordering component contract.
- Migration0033 precedes the dependent application; old rows and old-shaped inserts remain valid. The plan explicitly identifies the rollback trap in the old kg/szt-only readers after master opak/box data exists, and forbids destructive rollback.
